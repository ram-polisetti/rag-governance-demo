# Hiring Policy
## Scope
How the company runs structured hiring for all full-time roles.
## Interview panels
Every hiring panel must include at least 3 interviewers from at least two
different teams. No offer may be extended without panel consensus recorded
in the hiring system.
## Structured scorecards
Interviewers must submit structured scorecards within 24 hours of the
interview, rating the candidate on the pre-defined competencies for the
role. Hiring decisions may not be based on unstructured notes or memory.
## Offer approval
Offers require written approval from the hiring manager and HR. Any offer
above the compensation band midpoint requires VP approval.
