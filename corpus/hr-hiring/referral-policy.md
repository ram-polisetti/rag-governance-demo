# Employee Referral Policy
## Scope
Referral bonuses for full-time hires referred by current employees.
## Bonus amount
A $2,000 referral bonus is paid for each referred candidate who is hired
into a full-time role, except for executive hires which follow a separate
schedule.
## Payout timing
The bonus is paid after the referred hire completes 90 days of continuous
employment. If the hire leaves before 90 days, no bonus is paid.
## Exclusions
Hiring managers may not claim referral bonuses for hires into their own
teams. HR staff administering the program are not eligible.
