# Equal Opportunity Policy
## Scope
Applies to all employment decisions: hiring, promotion, compensation, and
termination.
## Non-discrimination
The company provides equal opportunity regardless of race, color, religion,
sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other characteristic protected
by law. Employment decisions must be based on job-related qualifications.
## Job descriptions
All job descriptions must be reviewed for biased or exclusionary language
before posting. Requirements listed must be genuine occupational
qualifications for the role.
## Accommodations
Candidates and employees may request reasonable accommodations in the
interview process or workplace. Requests are handled confidentially by HR
within 10 business days.
