# Compensation Bands Policy
## Scope
How the company sets and applies salary bands for all leveled roles.
## Bands
Each level has a published band. For example, the L4 band is $95,000 to
$130,000 base salary. Bands are reviewed annually against market data.
## Offer placement
Offers within the band require hiring-manager and HR approval. Any offer
above the band midpoint requires additional VP approval with written
business justification.
## Salary history
The company does not ask candidates for salary history at any stage of the
process, consistent with applicable law.
