# Background Check Policy
## Scope
Background checks for candidates in roles with financial, safety, or data
access responsibilities.
## Validity
A completed background check is valid for 12 months. Re-checks are required
for internal transfers into in-scope roles if the last check is older than
12 months.
## Candidate rights
Candidates are notified before a check is run and receive a copy of the
report. If the company intends to take adverse action based on the results,
the candidate must be notified at least 5 business days in advance and given
a chance to respond.
## Confidentiality
Background check results are stored separately from personnel files with
restricted HR access only.
