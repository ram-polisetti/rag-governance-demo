# Data Retention Policy
## Scope
How long the company keeps customer and operational records.
## Retention periods
Customer account records are retained for 7 years after the last account
activity. Transaction logs are retained for 7 years. Marketing contact data
is retained until consent is withdrawn.
## Deletion requests
Verified deletion requests are honored within 30 days. Backups containing
deleted data are purged on the next backup rotation cycle, at most 90 days
after the request.
## Legal holds
Retention periods are suspended for records under legal hold. Legal must
approve any deletion of held records.
