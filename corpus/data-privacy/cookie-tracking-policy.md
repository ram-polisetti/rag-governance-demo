# Cookie and Tracking Policy
## Scope
How the company uses cookies and similar tracking on its websites and apps.
## Consent
Consent is required before setting any non-essential cookies. Essential
cookies needed for security and core functionality do not require consent.
## Opt-out
Users may withdraw consent at any time through the preference center.
Opt-outs are honored immediately across all company properties.
## Third parties
Third-party trackers are listed in the public cookie register with their
purpose and data retention. New trackers require privacy review before
deployment.
