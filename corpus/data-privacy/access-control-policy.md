# Access Control Policy
## Scope
Who may access company systems and customer data, and how that access is
reviewed.
## Least privilege
Access is granted on a least-privilege basis: employees receive only the
permissions required for their role. Standing administrator access is
prohibited; elevated access is time-bound and ticketed.
## Access reviews
Managers must review their teams' access every quarter and revoke anything
no longer needed. Unused credentials are disabled after 45 days of
inactivity.
## Devices
Production customer data must never be stored on personal devices. Lost or
stolen company devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
