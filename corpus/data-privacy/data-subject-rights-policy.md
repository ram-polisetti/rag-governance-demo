# Data Subject Rights Policy
## Scope
How the company handles requests from individuals exercising their data
rights (access, correction, deletion, portability).
## Response time
Data subject access requests (DSARs) must be acknowledged within 5 business
days and fulfilled within 30 days of receipt.
## Identity verification
The requester's identity must be verified before any personal data is
disclosed. Verification uses at least two factors already on file.
## Fees
Requests are fulfilled free of charge. Manifestly unfounded or excessive
requests may be refused with a written explanation.
