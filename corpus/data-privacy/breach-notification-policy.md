# Breach Notification Policy
## Scope
What happens when a data breach is suspected or confirmed.
## Internal reporting
Any employee who suspects a breach must report it to the Data Protection
Officer within 24 hours of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate or
contain the breach alone.
## Regulator notification
Where notification is legally required, regulators must be notified within
72 hours of the company becoming aware of the breach.
## Individual notification
Affected individuals are notified without undue delay when the breach is
likely to result in high risk to their rights, with a description of the
breach and recommended protective steps.
