# Carrier Selection Policy
## Scope
How the company approves and reviews freight carriers for all modes.
## Approval criteria
A carrier is approved only if it meets all of: on-time delivery score of 98%
or higher over the trailing 90 days, cargo claims ratio below 0.5%, minimum
$1M cargo insurance, and two verifiable customer references.
## Scorecard reviews
Approved carriers are re-scored quarterly. A carrier falling below 98%
on-time delivery for two consecutive quarters is placed on probation; a
third consecutive miss removes approval.
## Spot carriers
Spot carriers may be used for surge volume with manager approval, but any
spot carrier used more than 3 times in a quarter must go through full
approval before its fourth load.
