# Hazardous Materials Handling Policy
## Scope
Applies to all warehouse staff who receive, store, pick, or ship hazardous
materials, including Class 3 flammable liquids, Class 8 corrosives, and
Division 4.1 flammable solids.
## Segregation and storage requirements
Class 3 flammable liquids must be stored at least 10 meters from oxidizers
(Class 5.1) and from any ignition source. Incompatible hazard classes must
never share a spill-containment pallet. Segregation follows the DOT
segregation table posted at each hazmat bay.
## PPE
Minimum PPE for hazmat handling: chemical-resistant gloves, splash goggles,
and steel-toe boots. A face shield is required when transferring liquids
between containers.
## Spill response
Spill kits are located at the end of every hazmat aisle. For spills under
5 liters, trained staff may contain and absorb the spill, then file an
incident report within 24 hours. Larger spills: evacuate the aisle and call
the EHS hotline immediately.
## Placarding and documentation
Every outbound hazmat shipment requires DOT placards matching the hazard
class and a shipper's declaration signed by a certified hazmat employee.
Declarations are retained for 2 years.
