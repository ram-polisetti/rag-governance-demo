# Returns and Recall Policy
## Scope
Customer returns, defective-product holds, and product recalls.
## Returns
Customers may return unused product within 30 days with an RMA number.
Returned product is inspected and graded before it re-enters sellable stock.
## Recalls
On receiving a recall notice, the affected lot must be quarantined within
4 hours across all sites, and the recall coordinator must be notified within
1 hour. No quarantined product may ship for any reason.
## Recall classes
Class I (serious health risk), Class II (reversible risk), and Class III
(low risk) follow the same quarantine clock; communication urgency differs.
## Traceability
Lot-level traceability records are retained for 7 years to support
recall scope determination.
