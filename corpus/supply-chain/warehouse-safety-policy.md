# Warehouse Safety Policy
## Scope
Everyone working in or visiting the distribution center.
## Forklift operations
Maximum forklift speed inside the warehouse is 8 km/h. Forklifts must stop
and sound the horn at every blind intersection. Only certified operators may
drive; certifications are renewed every 3 years.
## Pedestrian safety
Pedestrians must use marked walkways and wear high-visibility vests in all
operational areas. Pedestrians always have the right of way.
## Racking and storage requirements
All pallet racking is inspected quarterly. Damaged racking is tagged out of
service immediately and may not be used until repaired and re-inspected.
## Incident reporting
All injuries and near misses are reported to the shift supervisor within
1 hour and entered into the safety log the same day.
