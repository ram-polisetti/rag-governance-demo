# Customs Documentation Policy
## Scope
All cross-border shipments handled by the company.
## Required documents
Every international shipment needs a commercial invoice, a packing list,
and accurate HS codes for each line item. Missing HS codes are the most
common cause of customs delays.
## AES filing
An AES filing is required for any export shipment valued above $2,500.
The filing must be accepted before the goods leave the warehouse.
## ISF 10+2
For ocean imports, the ISF 10+2 filing must be submitted at least 24 hours
before vessel loading at the foreign port. Late ISF filings risk penalties
of up to $5,000 per violation.
## Record retention
Customs records are retained for 5 years from the date of import or export.
