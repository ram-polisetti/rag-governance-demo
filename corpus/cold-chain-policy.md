# Cold Chain Policy
## Scope
Covers all temperature-controlled storage and transport of pharmaceutical and
food products across the distribution network.
## Temperature ranges
Pharmaceutical products must be kept at 2°C to 8°C at all times. Frozen goods
must be kept at -18°C or below. Storage zones are mapped and qualified
annually; no product may be stored outside a qualified zone.
## Monitoring
Calibrated data loggers record zone temperature every 15 minutes. Alerts
fire when any reading leaves the qualified range.
## Temperature excursions
Any excursion lasting more than 30 minutes requires the affected product to
be quarantined immediately and reviewed by QA before release. Excursions are
logged with logger data attached; repeated excursions trigger a CAPA.
## Transport
Reefer trailers must be pre-cooled to the target range before loading, and
drivers must confirm the set point on the bill of lading at pickup.
