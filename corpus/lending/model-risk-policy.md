# Model Risk Policy
## Scope
Governance of statistical and machine-learning models used in credit
decisions.
## Validation
Credit models must be independently validated before first use and
revalidated at least annually, or sooner if performance degrades.
## Challenger models
Challenger models run in shadow mode alongside production models for at
least 3 months before promotion. Promotion requires documented
outperformance on the validation set and sign-off from model risk.
## Inventory
All models in use are recorded in the model inventory with owner, purpose,
validation date, and limitations.
