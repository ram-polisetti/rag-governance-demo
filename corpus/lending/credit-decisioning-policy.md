# Credit Decisioning Policy
## Scope
How consumer credit applications are evaluated and decided.
## Decision timing
Applicants must receive a decision within 5 business days of a completed
application. Incomplete applications pause the clock until the missing
information is received.
## Dual review
Applications for amounts above $250,000 require review by two credit
officers. Disagreements are escalated to the credit committee.
## Documentation
Every decision must record the factors considered and the data sources
used, retained with the application file for 7 years.
