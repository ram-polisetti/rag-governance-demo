# Adverse Action Notice Policy
## Scope
Notices sent when credit is denied or offered on less favorable terms.
## Timing and content
A written adverse action notice must be sent within 30 days of a completed
application. The notice must state the action taken and the specific
reasons for it — up to 4 specific reasons, not vague generalities.
## Credit score disclosure
If a credit score was used, the notice must include the score, the scoring
model, and the key factors that adversely affected it.
## Record retention
Copies of adverse action notices are retained for 7 years.
