# Fair Lending Policy
## Scope
Ensuring credit decisions are free from unlawful discrimination.
## Prohibited bases
No applicant may be treated less favorably on a prohibited basis under the
Equal Credit Opportunity Act, including race, color, religion, national
origin, sex, marital status, or age.
## Pricing exceptions
Any pricing exception from standard rate sheets must be documented with a
specific business justification and reviewed quarterly for disparate
impact.
## Monitoring
Lending outcomes are analyzed quarterly for disparities across protected
groups. Findings are reported to the compliance committee.
