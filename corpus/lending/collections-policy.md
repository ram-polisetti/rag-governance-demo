# Collections Policy
## Scope
How past-due accounts are contacted and worked.
## Contact hours
Collection calls may only be placed between 8am and 9pm in the borrower's
local time. No more than 3 contact attempts per account per week.
## Cease communication
A written request to cease communication must be honored within 2 business
days, except for notices required by law.
## Prohibited practices
Collectors must not threaten, misrepresent the debt, or contact third
parties about the debt except as permitted by law.
